Total Corpus size: ~1.2 million tokens
This supplementary document provides a lengthy, structured sample of the primary legal texts
that constitute the 1.2-million-token corpus used to train and evaluate the Legalyze platform.

Files included in the full corpus:
  1. Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan (1973)
  2. Pakistan Penal Code (PPC) (1860)
  3. Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) (1898)

The sections below contain extensive verbatim text extracts from each statutory code to 
demonstrate the density, hierarchical formatting (Parts, Chapters, Sections, and Articles),
and procedural terminology characteristic of the Pakistani legal framework.
